About This List

The 5 Star Demon List is a community opinion-based ranking of the current hardest 5 star osu!mania maps in the ranked section (counting for only rice difficulty).

As of currently, it only ranks the top 75.

About Placements

Exact placements on the list are decided by the difficulty rating system / difficulty table used on this website, the vDelta Difficulty Table (VDT for short). Two maps that have the same VDT are placed exactly on the list depending on very slight difficulty differences, or consistency of difficulty between different players (how many players may be likely to agree that one is harder than the other). For example, more gimmicky maps are typically placed lower among maps of the same VDT.
